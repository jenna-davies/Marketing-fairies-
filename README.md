Marketing Fairies 🧚
For consultants, coaches, and team leaders who share their expertise every day.

Everything you do is content. Everything you're busy with, you're an expert in.

But teaching people to make content for you is going to slow you down. Nobody's got your standards. Nobody's as good as you at what you do.

So let your work speak for itself.

The Marketing Fairies are designed to take your advice, your expertise, and turn that into engagement. Take what you're already teaching and turn that into awareness. If you're solving problems every day and giving valuable insights to your clients, these systems will help you scale purely by taking your expertise and distributing it to the world.


Why You Should Trust Me
I quit my full-time job as global marketing manager at 27 and tripled my income. I built a course. Made my first million. Hired a team. Built an agency. Built tech. Scaled my services. Rebuilt the tech.

Over that decade, I learned one thing: the patterns repeat.

Every week, the same work happens over and over. Extract the gold from calls. Document the transformation. Find the next opportunity. Structure the learning. Build the strategy.

So I systemised it. Five fairies. Five systems trained on a decade of coaching, consulting, content creation, and my own marketing experience.


The Five Fairies
🧚Content Fairy. Takes your calls and extracts what's shareable. The frameworks. The quotes. The stories. Turns them into content ready to post.

🧚Proof Fairy. Documents your client transformations automatically. Builds case studies. Creates testimonials. Proof ready to use.

🧚Scope Fairy. Listens for what your clients want next. Finds the expansion opportunities you're missing. Phase two revenue hiding in plain sight.

🧚Learning Fairy. Structures your coaching into learning materials that actually work. Homework gets done. Results stick.

🧚YouTube Fairy. Builds your complete YouTube strategy. Not random videos. A strategy.


⏰The Time You Get Back⏰
Content Fairy: 3-5 hours per week
Proof Fairy: 4-6 hours per client
Scope Fairy: Hours of guessing what to sell next
Learning Fairy: 2-3 hours per client
YouTube Fairy: 40 hours of strategy work

Total: Roughly 2 days back per week.

That's the capacity of a $150k hire. Without the hiring cost.


🚨Critical: Give Them Your Business Context🚨
The fairies work best when they're aligned with your business objectives and your vision.

When you set up the system, you need to tell them:

What your business actually does
Who your ideal client is
What problems you solve
Your unique perspective or philosophy
Your positioning in the market

The more context you give about YOUR vision and YOUR business focus, the better the fairies work.

They're not generic tools. They're built to amplify YOUR worldview and YOUR expertise. But they need to know what that is.

Read GETTING-STARTED.md for exactly how to do this.


🪞Important: They Learn Your Voice
The fairies don't produce generic AI content.

They learn how you speak. Then they speak back in YOUR voice.

Your content sounds like you. Your case studies read like you wrote them. Your strategy reflects your thinking.

The more you use them, the more their output sounds like you, not like AI.

Read HOW-THEY-LEARN.md to understand how this works.

Questions
Do the fairies store my data? No. They work with what you feed them. Nothing is stored.

Will they work for my business? If you have client calls, sessions, or engagements where you're sharing expertise, yes. If you sell products, no.

What if I've never used AI before? The prompts are simple. Feed the fairy your work. Ask her to do something. She does it.

What if I don't know what to prompt them with? We've included 50+ prompt examples. Start there.

Do the fairies work for every business? No. They work best when you're clear about your business objectives, your vision, and what you actually do. The better you define your focus, the better they work.
